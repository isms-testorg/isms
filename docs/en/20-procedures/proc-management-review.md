# Management Review Procedure

This document is part of the information security management system of dev-test. It is maintained under version control; the signed git history and the release packs are its change record.

## 1. Purpose

This procedure ensures that top management evaluates the continuing suitability, adequacy, effectiveness, and direction of the dev-test ISMS and makes resourced decisions.

## 2. Scope

It applies to the full ISMS scope, including context, interested parties, risks, objectives, controls, incidents, audits, suppliers, resources, and improvement actions. The review considers distributed work, employees and contractors, GitHub-centric delivery, customer-data processing, and supporting SaaS/cloud dependencies.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

The managing director chairs the review and approves decisions. The ISMS owner prepares the agenda, inputs, minutes, actions, and follow-up. The CTO reports engineering and security performance; the engineering lead reports development and release risks; the IT lead reports identity, endpoint, backup, supplier, and service matters. Other owners attend for relevant topics and provide accurate inputs.

## 4. Requirements

The ISMS owner schedules a review at least annually and when a material incident, legal change, organisational change, or scope change occurs. Inputs include prior actions, context and interested-party changes, risks and treatment, objectives and measurements, audit and compliance results, incidents and corrective actions, supplier performance, resource adequacy, security trends, and improvement opportunities. Participants evaluate trends and residual risk, then approve decisions on policy or scope changes, objectives, treatment, resources, priorities, and improvements. Outputs are dated minutes, decisions, owners, due dates, accepted residual risks, and escalations. The ISMS owner publishes approved minutes and tracks actions to closure.

## 5. Exceptions

If a required input is unavailable, the ISMS owner records the gap and its effect rather than inventing a result. The managing director may defer a decision only with an owner, due date, interim treatment, and recorded risk acceptance. Material unresolved risks are escalated to the managing director and CTO.

## 6. Monitoring and compliance

The ISMS owner monitors attendance, input completeness, decision closure, overdue actions, objective performance, and repeat issues. Action status is reviewed at least quarterly and reported in the next management review. Missing reviews or unimplemented decisions become nonconformities or corrective actions. This procedure is reviewed every 24 months or after a governance or scope change.

## 7. References

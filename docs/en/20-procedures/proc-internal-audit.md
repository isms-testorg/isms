# Internal Audit Procedure

This document is part of the information security management system of dev-test. It is maintained under version control; the signed git history and the release packs are its change record.

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines independent, risk-based internal audits that determine whether the dev-test ISMS conforms to its requirements and is implemented and maintained effectively for demonstration purposes.

## 2. Scope

It covers the ISMS scope, clauses 4–10, selected Annex A controls, registers, procedures, documented information, operational evidence, objectives, risks, suppliers, and relevant GitHub and cloud processes. It applies to all teams and locations within scope.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

The ISMS owner maintains the audit programme and appoints a competent auditor who is independent of the activity audited where practicable. Process owners provide access and evidence. The auditor plans, samples, interviews, records findings, and reports results. The CTO and IT or engineering leads support technical audits. The managing director receives the report and approves priorities and resources; owners address findings without changing the audit conclusion.

## 4. Requirements

At least annually, the ISMS owner sets audit scope, criteria, objectives, methods, timing, competence, and sampling using risk, prior findings, changes, incidents, and objective performance. The auditor reviews evidence, interviews responsible people, tests selected records and controls, and records conformity, observations, and nonconformities with traceable evidence. The auditee may provide factual corrections; the auditor approves the final report. Inputs are the audit programme, SoA, risks, objectives, prior reports, policies, procedures, and records. Outputs are the plan, working papers, report, findings, corrective actions, and follow-up result. Audit records are retained in the ISMS repository with appropriate classification.

## 5. Exceptions

An audit may be rescheduled for a documented operational emergency, but the ISMS owner records the reason, new date, and interim risk treatment. An auditor may not audit work for which they were the primary author or approver unless no practical alternative exists and the conflict is disclosed and mitigated.

## 6. Monitoring and compliance

The ISMS owner tracks programme completion, overdue reports, repeat findings, closure evidence, and auditor independence. Findings are escalated to the managing director when high risk, repeated, overdue, or potentially reportable. Results feed management review and corrective action. The procedure is reviewed every 24 months or after a major audit, scope, standard, or organisational change.

## 7. References

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause 9.2; the audit programme; the SoA, risk, objective, document-control, and corrective-action records; and the repository validator and release process.

## 8. Related Annex A controls

None directly. This document satisfies a requirement from clauses 4 to 10 of the standard.

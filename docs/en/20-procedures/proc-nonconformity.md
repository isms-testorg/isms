# Nonconformity and Corrective Action Procedure

This document is part of the information security management system of dev-test. It is maintained under version control; the signed git history and the release packs are its change record.

## 1. Purpose

This procedure ensures that deviations from ISMS, legal, contractual, policy, or control requirements are contained, investigated, corrected, and reviewed for effectiveness.

## 2. Scope

It applies to findings from internal audits, incidents, access reviews, monitoring, management review, supplier reviews, validation checks, complaints, and operational work across the dev-test ISMS scope.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

Anyone may report a suspected nonconformity to the ISMS owner. The ISMS owner records and classifies it, assigns an owner, and verifies closure. The relevant process owner contains the issue and proposes correction and root-cause action. The CTO or IT/engineering lead approves technical treatment in their area. The managing director approves acceptance of material residual risk and receives significant or overdue matters. The auditor or reviewer who raised a finding verifies effectiveness where independent verification is practicable.

## 4. Requirements

The record states the requirement, objective evidence, date, affected asset or process, impact, immediate correction, root cause, corrective action, owner, priority, due date, and escalation path. The owner first contains or corrects the issue, then analyses systemic causes and checks whether similar issues exist. Actions are risk-based, assigned, resourced, and tracked. Closure requires evidence that the action was implemented and an effectiveness check after an appropriate operating period. Inputs are findings, records, incidents, obligations, and risk assessments. Outputs are the controlled finding record, correction, corrective-action plan, verification result, residual-risk decision, and lessons learned.

## 5. Exceptions

Due dates may be extended only by the ISMS owner with documented reason, interim control, revised date, and risk-owner approval. A person may not approve their own finding closure without independent review. High-risk, repeat, legally sensitive, or customer-impacting matters are escalated immediately to the CTO and managing director.

## 6. Monitoring and compliance

The ISMS owner reports open, overdue, repeat, high-risk, and ineffective actions at least quarterly and in management review. Trends are used to update risks, objectives, procedures, and training. Closure evidence is sampled and retained according to document-control requirements. The procedure is reviewed every 24 months or after a major finding or change to obligations.
